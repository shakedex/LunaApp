First release of the native Luna app.

- **Windows:** `Luna-2026.10.0-windows-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon:** `Luna-2026.10.0-macos.dmg`, signed and notarized.

Reads ARRIRAW, ARRICORE, X-OCN, XAVC, R3D, Blackmagic RAW, Cinema RAW Light, XF-AVC, XF-HEVC, N-RAW, ProRes, ProRes RAW, H.264 and HEVC. Writes the report as PDF, HTML and CSV.

The `.zip` and `appcast` files are for Luna's built-in updates.
