- **Windows:** `Luna-2026.11.0-windows-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon:** `Luna-2026.11.0-macos.dmg`, signed and notarized.

New: **Camera look** for Sony and ARRI. With Camera look on in Settings (`--camera-look` on the command line), the stills use the look the camera recorded: the LUT in a VENICE 2 X-OCN or BURANO XAVC clip, and the look of an ALEXA 35 (ARRIRAW, ARRICORE, HDE, ProRes) or ALEXA Mini LF (ARRIRAW, HDE, ProRes) clip, as the camera's monitor output showed it. Each clip names its look, for example "5110 Nature.alf4".

New: **Texture** for ALEXA 35 clips (ProRes, ARRICORE, ARRIRAW, HDE), for example "K445 Default", on the clip card and as a CSV column.

Fixed: ALEXA 65 `.ari` stills had a magenta cast. They now match ARRI Reference Tool.

Fixed: an `.ari` or `.arx` frame sequence with missing frames showed a TC out and duration that were too short. Both now run to the last frame's own timecode, and the clip needs attention, naming the missing frames (for example "Frames missing from the sequence: 0078623–0078929 (307 of 480)").

Fixed: PDF pages that only list other files or unreadable files no longer carry the last camera and reel in the page header.

Fixed: after opening Luna with `Luna report <folder>` and going **Back**, **Create report** ignored the fields and settings changed in the window. It now uses them; options given on the command line still win.
