- **Windows:** `Luna-2026.10.3-windows-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon:** `Luna-2026.10.3-macos.dmg`.

New: **Settings → Colour** for the thumbnails: **Rec.709**, **None** (the camera's log) or a `.cube` **LUT**. On the command line: `--colour rec709|none|lut` and `--lut <file>`.

New: **Camera look** in Settings. A Blackmagic RAW clip recorded with "Apply LUT in File" uses its own LUT for the stills.

New: **Settings → Show problems in reports**, off by default: clip warnings, the "Need attention" count and unreadable files in the report (`--problems` on the command line).

New: ARRIRAW HDE from the ALEXA 35 and ALEXA Mini LF, in MXF and as `.arx` frame sequences.

New: ALEXA Mini LF and ALEXA Mini ARRIRAW in MXF get thumbnails.

New: Sony XAVC H Intra (HEVC in MXF) from the BURANO.

New: `.ari` and `.arx` frame sequences are one clip each, with timecode, recording date, lens, T-stop, ND, white balance and EI from the camera.

New: Blackmagic RAW scene and take from the camera's slate, as **Slate** on the clip card.

New: Canon EOS C200 Cinema RAW Light gets thumbnails.

Changed: Luna checks for updates every time it opens, not only once a day.

Changed: Sony VENICE lenses are named from their /i serial (for example "Cooke S7/i 85mm").

Changed: a CinemaDNG sequence is one clip that says "CinemaDNG isn't supported yet", instead of "No camera clips in this folder".

Changed: a reel shared by two folders names the source folder by its own name instead of "(.)".

Fixed: switching "Also save on the source drive" or "Show problems in reports" and then saving other Settings edits switched it back.

Fixed: Sony VENICE X-OCN XT clips had blown-out thumbnails.

Fixed: Atomos Ninja V ProRes RAW failed on every frame (Windows).

Fixed: ARRIRAW HDE in MXF was read as plain ARRIRAW and failed.

Fixed: ALEXA Mini LF clips showed no camera serial or ND.

Fixed: some Sony FX9 clips showed "Sony" as the camera and a code as the lens. They now show the model, serial and lens name.

Fixed: Avid OP-Atom MXF was reported as damaged, and its sound files showed up as clips.
