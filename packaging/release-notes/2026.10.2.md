- **Windows:** `Luna-2026.10.2-windows-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon:** `Luna-2026.10.2-macos.dmg`, signed and notarized.

New: every report run writes a log file: what Luna found in each clip, which decoder read it, the levels and values behind each thumbnail, and the numbers behind each warning. **Settings → Show logs** opens the folder. Send the log for the run when you report a problem.

New: iPhone clips show lens, focal length (35 mm equivalent) and f-stop.

New: Blackmagic Video Assist clips take their camera and reel from the recorder, and show the recorder's scene, shot and take as **Slate** on the clip card.

Changed: the CSV has three new columns after Reel: Scene, Shot and Take. Scripts that read columns by position need updating.

Fixed: iPhone clips at 25 fps whose frame timing varies by a fraction of a millisecond were reported as "The frame rate varies within the clip" with no frame rate. They now read 25 fps.

Fixed: clips shot in portrait on a phone had sideways thumbnails.

Fixed: an MXF recording that was cut off before it finished (power loss, card pulled) had no frame count or thumbnails. It now shows the frames the file holds.
