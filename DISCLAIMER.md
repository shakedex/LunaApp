## Local Processing

Luna runs entirely on your computer and never uploads footage, metadata or
reports anywhere. It doesn't change your source files. With **Also save on the
source drive** on in Settings (off by default), it writes a copy of each
report to a `Luna` folder at the root of the source drive.
