---
title: Requirements
description: A Chromium browser on a desktop or laptop.
---

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, or another Chromium browser, on a desktop or laptop. Phones and tablets
aren't supported.

Luna reads your card straight off the disk with the
[File System Access API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/File_System_Access_API).
Firefox, Safari, and mobile browsers don't have it.

WebCodecs is optional — without it, thumbnails decode in software, which is slower.
